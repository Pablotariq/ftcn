F.T.C.N. Ediciones is a music and publishing label operating between Madrid and Amsterdam. Founded in Hamburg in 2017 by Javi, Miguel (Keftagine) and Pablo (Feet).

F.T.C.N. releases music from its immediate surroundings, not sticking to a particular genre or style but bound together by an editorial line guided by DIY philosophy and sonic experimentation. The label stands for physical formats in all their forms: among them cassettes, vinyl, CDs, fanzines and books.

As a band, Fetuccini embraces dungeon soundscapes: heavy rhythms, hypnotic loops and endless echoes.

If you'd like to buy a tape or fanzine, book us to play at your castle or just say hi, drop us a line at ftcnediciones@gmail.com.
